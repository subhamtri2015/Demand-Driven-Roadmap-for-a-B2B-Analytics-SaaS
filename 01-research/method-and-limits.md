# Method and Limits

Independent analysis of public information; not affiliated with or endorsed by Metabase.

- 👍 reactions are a proxy for demand from vocal users, not customers or total user demand. Reactions can change over time.
- A closed issue does not mean shipped because close reason is not captured. Closed with a milestone is only a shipped proxy, not proof of release.
- Labels are public metadata and may change in meaning or application. Metabot-label share may reflect labeling practice as well as topic mix.
- Product-area labels can be multiple per request; area totals can double-count requests.
- Merged-PR counts include bots and backports and are not velocity, throughput, or a measure of release quality.
- npm data records published versions, not private release decisions, adoption, usage, or support windows. Downloads were not pulled.
- Public issue data does not expose feature usage events, customer outcomes, internal revenue, internal strategy, real sprint velocity, or Jira workflow state. Those are unavailable here; no results are claimed for them.
- Search API totals and issue state are snapshots. Data was pulled 2026-10-19; subsequent changes are not reflected.

Every RICE score, confidence/impact/effort value, success target, persona, and delivery estimate in this case study is an assumption or hypothesis, not a measured product result.
