# Hypotheses

Written before reviewing the processed outputs. These are independent, testable hypotheses about public signals, not claims about Metabase's internal strategy.

| ID | Hypothesis | Planned evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Demand is concentrated in a few requests. | Share of 👍 held by top decile and zero-👍 proportion. |
| H2 | AI-area requests attract more demand per request. | Mean 👍 for Metabot-labeled versus other requests; labeling is an imperfect area proxy. |
| H3 | More demand means more likely to be planned (milestone). | Milestone proportion by 👍 bucket. |
| H4 | Priority labels follow demand. | Priority-label proportion by 👍 bucket. |
| H5 | Requests that receive a milestone take longer to close than those without. | Median lead time among closed issues, grouped by milestone; association only. |

Verdicts and limitations are recorded in [analysis findings](../02-analysis/findings.md).
