# Definition of Ready

**Planned, not executed.** A story may be considered ready for a future planning discussion when:

- User problem and intended outcome are stated and linked to evidence or clearly marked as an assumption.
- Acceptance criteria are testable and edge cases are identified.
- Dependencies, privacy implications, and design/engineering questions are documented.
- Estimate is an assumption agreed with the delivery team, not a commitment.
- Scope fits the planned sprint and release constraints after team review.
