## Segment (hypothesis)

B2B SaaS teams embedding analytics into a product and seeking consistent brand expression. This segment is hypothetical and needs interview validation.

## Problem

Public issue #59237 requests CSS variables in SDK theming and appears among the high-reaction open requests in the processed public dataset. Public issue reactions are a vocal-user demand proxy, not customer evidence. The Superset PR context also illustrates theme precedence as an external integration concern; its status is unverified.

## Bets (proposed)

1. Specify a CSS-variable theming interface, including explicit precedence rules.
2. Validate integration constraints with embedded analytics developers before committing implementation scope.
3. Pair the proposed capability with preview, isolation, and instrumentation plans so a release can be evaluated.

## What we say no to

No claim of customer impact, adoption, or usage without telemetry and research. No promise that CSS variables solve all embedding-brand needs. No roadmap commitment from 👍 alone.

## What we only maintain

AI-area requests are visible in public labels, but this analysis does not establish strategic priority. Maintain awareness and handle case-by-case rather than claim an AI investment priority. Public lifecycle labels show incomplete planning signals, not internal workflow.
